Qabasat Uploader

Qabasat Uploader (shown on Google's consent screen as "OpenMontage Uploader") is a private tool that uploads videos to one YouTube channel: @shia-quran313. Only the channel's operator uses it. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to use it for their own channel through this page.

The videos it uploads present Quran commentary and sayings of Imam Ali and the Imams of Ahl al-Bayt, with sources named in each description.
