Qabasat Uploader

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026.

Who uses this tool

Qabasat Uploader (shown on Google's consent screen as "OpenMontage Uploader") is used only by the operator of the YouTube channel @shia-quran313, to manage that channel. It has no other users.

Google and YouTube data it accesses

  • youtube.upload: to upload the channel's own videos.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel's own details and the status of its videos, such as whether a video is private or public.
  • youtube: to make the channel's own uploaded videos public. This permission is broad, but the tool uses it only to change the visibility of videos it uploaded.

It does not read the operator's email, contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

The access tokens and the list of uploaded video IDs are stored only on the operator's own computer. There is no server and no database behind this tool.

The operator runs the tool with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) on that computer. The assistant can see the channel's video IDs, titles and status while it works. The access tokens are not given to it to read or share.

The data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone else, except that videos and their details are sent to YouTube because that is what the tool is for.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers and visitors

The tool collects nothing about people who watch the channel. These pages set no cookies and use no analytics of their own. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' technical data such as IP addresses.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google account permissions. Deleting the token files on the operator's computer removes the stored access.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the project's issues page.
